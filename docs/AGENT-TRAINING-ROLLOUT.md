# Field Revenue Agent Training Curriculum & Rollout Guide

**Document ID:** PSIRS-TRN-2026-V1  
**Target Group:** Candidate Field Revenue Agents, LGA Supervisors, Master Trainers  
**Prerequisites:** Completed NIN KYC, Referee Clearance, Android/iOS Smartphone  

---

## 1. Objectives of the Agent Training Programme

Under **Addendum §14 and §26**, field agents represent the government at the grassroots. Before receiving collection authorization, every agent must complete and pass all **12 interactive training modules** embedded within the platform.

---

## 2. The 12-Module Training Curriculum

| Module # | Module Title | Key Learning Objectives |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Introduction to PSIRS & Grassroots Revenue** | Plateau State revenue laws, ethics of revenue collection, taxpayer rights. |
| **02** | **The Agent Code of Conduct** | Prohibitions against cash handling, extortion, and unreceipted transactions. |
| **03** | **Operating the Agent PWA** | Installing the app, offline draft capture, session management, biometric sign-in. |
| **04** | **Taxpayer Registration & TIN Onboarding** | Individual vs Business TINs, collecting NIN, address & Ward mapping. |
| **05** | **The Revenue Catalogue & Rates** | Understanding fixed, formula, and self-assessable rates; LGA applicability. |
| **06** | **Payment Methods & Gateway Operation** | Point-of-Sale (POS), direct bank transfers, Remita RRR generation, USSD. |
| **07** | **Official Digital Receipts & Tamper Proofing** | Understanding QR verification, security codes, and SMS receipt delivery. |
| **08** | **Motor Vehicle Renewals & Clearances** | Plate lookup, ownership verification, vehicle renewal certificates. |
| **09** | **Mobile Thermal Printing in the Field** | Pairing Bluetooth ESC/POS printers, paper roll replacement, offline slips. |
| **10** | **Offline Mode & Synchronization** | Handling low/no connectivity areas, draft queue resolution, sync triggers. |
| **11** | **Commission Structure & Payout Rules** | 1.5% basis points calculation, 72-hour hold, wallet withdrawal requests. |
| **12** | **Dispute Resolution & Support Escalation** | Creating support tickets, handling unconfirmed payments, taxpayer support. |

---

## 3. Practical Field Assessment & Certification

1. **Self-Paced Mobile Learning:** The candidate agent completes modules directly in the PWA (`#/application`).
2. **Interactive Knowledge Check:** Each module ends with a comprehension quiz (minimum passing score: 80%).
3. **Field Supervisor Practical Evaluation:**
   - Candidate performs 1 simulated taxpayer registration.
   - Candidate executes 1 test assessment and mock payment.
   - Candidate pairs a Bluetooth thermal printer and prints a sample test receipt.
4. **Automated Clearance Trigger:**
   - Once all 12 modules are marked `COMPLETED` in `agent_training_progress`, the backend database automatically marks the training requirement cleared, allowing supervisor approval.
